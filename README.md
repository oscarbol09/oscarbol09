### 🙋 About Me

Computer Science graduate from Universidad de Córdoba (Colombia). Full-stack developer focused on building robust, scalable web applications for clients and personal projects. I work primarily with the **JavaScript/TypeScript** and **Python** ecosystems across frontend, backend, and systems development, with experience delivering complete solutions — from architecture design to production deployment.

I also build applied AI tools, integrating language models into real workflows (RAG, educational chatbots, document automation) and actively contribute to open-source software.

---

### 🔨 What I'm Working On

- **Freelance Development:** custom websites and web applications for clients, from landing pages to platforms with custom backends
- **EduRAG-Platform:** RAG-based educational platform allowing teachers to create chatbots trained on their own documents
- **AudioBard:** AI-powered multi-voice audiobook generator (Desktop GUI & CLI) with companion layout-aware PDF converter (`pdf2bard`)
- **Open Source Contributions:** contributing features, fixes, and automation to modern Linux systems and enterprise ERP ecosystems
